# skills

Small, copy-pasteable Claude skills.

## x-following-audit
Audit who you follow on X and get a colour-coded spreadsheet of accounts to cull (engagement bait, follow-farming, AI slop, inactive). No paid API; you scroll your own Following page.

**Use it:** paste [`x-following-audit/SKILL.md`](x-following-audit/SKILL.md) into Claude (Claude Code, desktop app or Cowork) with the [Claude in Chrome](https://claude.com/chrome) extension installed, and say "run this".
